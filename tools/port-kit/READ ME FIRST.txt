BLOODBORNE PC SEAMLESS CO-OP TEST KIT
=====================================

This is a test build of seamless co-op on Bloodborne PC, the native port: the game runs
natively instead of in shadPS4, usually much smoother and at 60 FPS. The co-op is the same as
in the shadPS4 kit. One player (the host) runs the servers; the other (the guest) only runs the
game.

BOTH PLAYERS MUST USE THIS KIT. Do not team up one player on this kit and one on the shadPS4
kit: that mix has not been tested yet.

YOU NEED
  - Your own Bloodborne.PC.Online folder (game 01.09), the same one you
    already play with. Keep the same mods enabled as the host. Nothing in it
    gets changed.
  - The VPN you already use with the host (Radmin VPN), connected.
  - A Vulkan 1.3 graphics card with a current driver, Windows 10 (1903+) or 11.
    Nothing else: Python and the libraries are inside the kit.

STEPS (guest)
  1. Unzip the zip straight into your Bloodborne.PC.Online folder, next to
     "LAUNCH - shadPS4 Launcher.bat". You get a SeamlessCoopPort folder and a few
     "... Port Co-op" .bat files beside your old ones.
  2. In SeamlessCoopPort, right-click "FIREWALL - Allow (run as admin)" and run it
     as administrator.
  3. Run "SETUP - Port Co-op". If you have the shadPS4 co-op kit (SeamlessCoop)
     it offers the same answers: just press Enter on each. Otherwise:
       Bloodborne.PC.Online folder   Enter (it finds the folder it is in)
       host or guest                 guest
       Host IP                       the host's 26.x.x.x address from Radmin VPN
       Account name / password       the same as in the shadPS4 kit, or pick new
                                     ones and tell the host so they can register them
       Party                         exactly what the host tells you
       P2P UDP port                  Enter (auto)
       Frame rate                    Enter (60)
  4. Wait for the host to say the servers are up. Optional check: open
     http://HOST-IP:20443/status in a browser; it should say "ok": true.
  5. Run "LAUNCH - Port Co-op", load your character, and wait. You are pulled into
     the host's world automatically after a few seconds. No bells needed.
     The first start takes longer (it prepares the game and builds its shader cache).

STEPS (host)
  The same as the guest, with "host" in step 3 and your own VPN IP as Host IP. Then:
  - Run "SERVER - Port Co-op Host" and leave both windows open. When the shadPS4 kit
    (SeamlessCoop) is beside this one it starts that kit's servers, so every account
    registered there works here too. If those servers are already running, it says so
    and starts nothing.
  - Register new accounts with "SERVER - Port Co-op Register User".

SAVES
  SETUP copies your saves once, from the shadPS4 co-op kit (SeamlessCoop) when you
  have it, else from your install. From then on this kit keeps its own saves in
  SeamlessCoopPort\user\savedata: progress made here does not go back to shadPS4.
  To copy them again, delete SeamlessCoopPort\user\savedata and run SETUP.

PARTY DEATH (AS IN ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN)
  Always on.
  - A killing blow puts you down at 1 HP instead of killing you while your partner
    stands on the same map. Enemies leave you alone while you are down.
  - Your partner revives you by striking you: walk up and press R1 or R2 right next to
    you. Each strike deals their weapon's attack rating (R2 half again): they must deal
    your max HP the first time, twice that the next, four times after that (this starts
    over after 3 minutes without going down, or at a lamp). A stronger weapon revives
    sooner. Progress drains if they stop striking.
  - Hold Circle for 3 seconds to give up: you die as before and wake at your lamp.
  - When everyone on a map is down, everyone there dies, drops their echoes and
    wakes at their own last lamp with the map reset. Alone on a map, a death is such
    a wipe at once.

BLOODSTAINS
  No enemy steals a bloodstain any more (no blue-eyed enemy holding your echoes): your
  bloodstain stays where you died until you pick it up, in co-op and solo.

CONTROLS AND SETTINGS
  - In the game, Insert (or L3+R3 on a gamepad) opens the port's menu: resolution,
    upscaler (FSR, DLSS on RTX cards), effects. "Apply and restart" keeps co-op on.
  - Keyboard: WASD move, arrows camera, Space Cross, Left Shift Circle, E Square,
    Q Triangle, 1/3 L1/R1, R/F L2/R2, Z/C L3/R3, I/K/J/L d-pad, Enter Options.
  - Always start with "LAUNCH - Port Co-op". BLauncher.exe and Bloodborne.exe in the kit
    are the port's own launcher and quick start: starting the game from them plays
    WITHOUT co-op. Updates come as a new kit from the host, never from the launcher.

PROBLEMS
  - Black screen at start: delete SeamlessCoopPort\user\shader and start again.
  - If something goes wrong, send the host these files from SeamlessCoopPort\user\log:
    shad_log.txt and console.txt (and the .prev ones from the start before).
  - When the host sends a newer kit, unzip it the same way (overwrite) and run
    "SETUP - Port Co-op" again (Enter keeps every answer).
